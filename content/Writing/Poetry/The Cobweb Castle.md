***The Cobweb castle***
Sits on a knoll in the quiet forest
it is small and it seems
like frosting to slip off the crest

pressed by the junkyard around
which moans, groans, surrounds the home
in a pile of sticks and stones
left by a forgotten troll

he labored a century polishing floors
forgetting the sad sagging rafters
or to hang the doors.
